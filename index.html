Lightsteel Hockey
Training Centre

A purpose-built hockey development facility at the new Langley Events Centre rinks. Everything under one roof.

Performance Gym

Racks, dumbbells, cables, plyometrics, and sprint space. Built for Triphasic training with up to 20 athletes at a time.

Synthetic Ice Centre

Full shooting lanes, puck machines, targets, video analysis, and boards. 42x25' of synthetic ice for year-round skills work.

Speed & Testing Lab

Laser timing systems, sprint lanes outside the doors, and jump testing. Objective data drives every training block.

Skatemill

On-site skating treadmill for stride analysis and technique work without needing ice time.
